A note for the curious

There's a whole world to discover when you start looking a little closer.

In the details of an old painting. In the streets of an unfamiliar city. In the sketch that didn't turn out quite right, but made you want to keep going.

unwakingart is for people who want to make more room for creativity in their lives.

You don't need to be an artist. You don't need to know where to begin. You just need a little curiosity.

Through creative hangouts, art study trips, and ways to explore art together, we make space for trying new things, discovering old things differently, and meeting people who are curious about the same things you are.

Come to make something. Come to see something. Come because you want to spend an afternoon a little differently.

There's no one right way to be creative. And there's always more to discover when you step a little outside the lines
